HEMS base map

Helicopter air medical bases across the United States, every operator, with the aircraft at each base.

How to read it
  • Bases are grouped and colored by the Part 135 certificate holder, which is the operator.
  • Tap the legend button in the map's title bar to see which color is which operator, and uncheck operators to show only the ones you want.
  • Tap any marker for the base name and aircraft.
