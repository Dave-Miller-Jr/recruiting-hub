## CO|Frisco
intro: Frisco is a mountain town of about 2,900 people in Summit County, Colorado, on the shore of Dillon Reservoir at about 9,000 feet. It is a base for skiers, with four major resorts close by.
family: Dillon Amphitheater, beside the Dillon marina, hosts concerts and community events through the summer ; Breckenridge holds a Fourth of July parade and an international snow sculpture competition each January ; Free Summit Stage buses run between Frisco, Silverthorne, Breckenridge and other towns
outdoors: Dillon Reservoir has an 18-mile paved path around its shore and is stocked with rainbow trout every year ; Frisco and Dillon both have marinas on the reservoir, with sailing and boat rentals ; Breckenridge, Copper Mountain, Keystone and Arapahoe Basin ski resorts are all close to town
food: Breckenridge's historic Main Street, about 20 minutes away, is lined with shops and restaurants ; Denver, about an hour and a half away, has big-city dining, museums and a large performing arts complex
sports: Denver has the Broncos, Rockies, Nuggets, Avalanche and Rapids ; Skiing and snowboarding are the local sports, with four resorts nearby
cost: Cost of living is well above both the U.S. and Colorado averages, driven mostly by very high housing costs.
climate: A high-mountain climate. Summer highs are in the low 70s, January highs are around 30, and snow generally falls from late September to mid-May.
vets: The VA medical center for the region is in Aurora, on the east side of Denver ; VA clinics are also in Englewood and Golden
around: Denver is about an hour and a half east on Interstate 70, and Denver International Airport is about two hours ; Eagle County Regional Airport, about an hour and 15 minutes west, has seasonal flights ; Bustang buses stop in Frisco between Denver and Grand Junction, and Summit Stage runs free local buses

## FL|Venice
intro: Venice is a city of about 25,000 people on Florida's Gulf Coast in Sarasota County, part of the Sarasota metropolitan area.
family: Venice is known for the fossilized shark teeth found on its beaches and holds an annual Shark's Tooth Festival ; Sarasota, about 35 minutes away, has the Ringling Museum of Art and the Mote Marine Laboratory aquarium ; Siesta Beach near Sarasota is a year-round draw
outdoors: Venetian Waterway Park is a 9.3-mile paved trail along both sides of the Intracoastal Waterway ; Myakka River State Park, about 30 minutes away, covers 37,000 acres with a canopy walkway, boardwalk and canoeing ; Oscar Scherer State Park, about 15 minutes away, has swimming, scrub jay habitat and the Legacy Trail
food: Sarasota, about 35 minutes away, has an opera house, theaters and a performing arts hall ; Tampa's historic Ybor City district is about an hour and 25 minutes away
sports: The Atlanta Braves hold spring training in North Port, about 30 minutes away ; The Baltimore Orioles hold spring training at Ed Smith Stadium in Sarasota ; Tampa, about an hour and 25 minutes away, has the Buccaneers, Lightning and Rays
cost: Overall cost of living is a little above the U.S. and Florida averages, with housing somewhat above average. Florida has no state income tax.
climate: Hot, humid summers with highs in the low 90s and mild winters with highs in the low to mid 70s. June through September are the wettest months, and the area can be affected by hurricanes.
vets: VA clinics are in Sarasota and Port Charlotte, part of the Bay Pines VA system ; MacDill Air Force Base in Tampa is about an hour and 35 minutes away
around: Sarasota-Bradenton International Airport, just north of downtown Sarasota, has service from several airlines ; Sarasota is about 35 minutes away and Tampa about an hour and 25 minutes ; Interstate 75 and U.S. 41, the Tamiami Trail, run through the city

## IA|Audubon
intro: Audubon is a small farm town of about 2,000 people and the county seat of Audubon County in western Iowa, roughly halfway between Des Moines and Omaha.
family: Albert the Bull, a 30-foot-tall, 45-ton statue, is the town's landmark ; Omaha, about an hour and 35 minutes away, has the Henry Doorly Zoo and Aquarium and the Durham Museum ; Des Moines, about an hour and 45 minutes away, has the Science Center of Iowa and a botanical garden
outdoors: Prairie Rose State Park near Harlan has a 218-acre lake and 7 miles of multi-use trails ; Prairie Rose Lake has two boat ramps and fishing for bass, bluegill, catfish and crappie
food: Des Moines has touring Broadway shows and concerts at its Civic Center ; Omaha is the other big city for dining and nightlife, about an hour and 35 minutes away
sports: The Iowa Cubs play minor league baseball in Des Moines ; The College World Series is held in Omaha every year
cost: Cost of living is well below both the U.S. and Iowa averages, and housing costs are far below the national average.
climate: Four distinct seasons. July highs are in the low 80s and January highs are in the upper 20s, with about 24 inches of snow a year.
vets: The VA medical center for central Iowa is in Des Moines ; A VA clinic is in Carroll ; Offutt Air Force Base is just south of Omaha
around: Des Moines is about an hour and 45 minutes away and Omaha about an hour and 35 minutes ; The nearest commercial airports are Des Moines International and Omaha's Eppley Airfield ; U.S. Route 71 runs through town and meets Interstate 80 near Brayton

## ID|Driggs
intro: Driggs is a small city of about 2,000 people and the seat of Teton County in eastern Idaho. It sits in the Teton Valley at about 6,100 feet, on the west side of the Teton Range.
family: Grand Teton National Park, just north of Jackson, Wyoming, covers about 310,000 acres including the Teton Range ; The Idaho Falls Zoo, about an hour and 40 minutes away, has more than 300 animals ; Jackson's town square, about an hour away, is surrounded by shops, restaurants and art galleries
outdoors: Grand Targhee Resort, about 12 miles away in Alta, Wyoming, averages more than 500 inches of snow a year ; The resort sits in the Caribou-Targhee National Forest ; The South Fork of the Snake River nearby is a well-known place to fish for wild trout
food: Jackson, about an hour away, has a dining and shopping district around its town square ; Idaho Falls, about an hour and 40 minutes away, has a downtown with restaurants and a farmers market
sports: The Idaho Falls Chukars play Pioneer League baseball in Idaho Falls ; Skiing and snowboarding at Grand Targhee are the local winter sports
cost: Cost of living is above both the U.S. and Idaho averages, mainly because of housing. Groceries and utilities run below the national average.
climate: Winters are cold and snowy, with January highs in the low 30s and about 69 inches of snow a year. Summers are warm and pleasant, with July highs in the low 80s.
vets: The VA has clinics in Idaho Falls, about an hour and 40 minutes away, and in Pocatello.
around: Jackson Hole Airport, about an hour and 10 minutes away, has year-round service from four major airlines ; Jackson is about an hour away, Rexburg about an hour and 10 minutes and Idaho Falls about an hour and 40 minutes ; State Highway 33 is the main road, and START buses connect Teton Valley with Jackson

## IL|Cahokia
intro: Cahokia is a community on the Illinois side of the Mississippi River across from St. Louis, part of the city of Cahokia Heights since 2021. St. Louis Downtown Airport is here, about 10 minutes from downtown St. Louis.
family: Cahokia Mounds State Historic Site has a museum and interpretive center ; The Saint Louis Zoo and the Missouri History Museum are in Forest Park in St. Louis ; The Church of the Holy Family in Cahokia is described as the oldest continuously active Catholic parish in the United States
outdoors: Cahokia Mounds, a UNESCO World Heritage Site about 20 minutes away, covers about 2,200 acres with roughly 80 mounds ; Monks Mound, the largest earthwork at the site, rises about 100 feet ; Frank Holten State Recreation Area is nearby along Interstate 255
food: Downtown St. Louis is about 10 minutes away for dining and nightlife ; Laclede's Landing, a cobblestone riverfront district north of the Gateway Arch, is known for restaurants and nightclubs
sports: St. Louis has the Cardinals, the Blues and St. Louis City SC ; The Gateway Grizzlies play baseball at their ballpark in neighboring Sauget
cost: Cost of living in Cahokia is well below the U.S. average, and housing costs are far below average. Illinois has a flat state income tax.
climate: Four seasons. July highs are near 90 and January highs are in the low 40s, with about 14 inches of snow in a typical year. Spring and fall are mild.
vets: The VA St. Louis health care system has two main campuses in St. Louis, on North Grand Boulevard and at Jefferson Barracks ; Scott Air Force Base, about 35 minutes away, also has a VA clinic
around: St. Louis Lambert International Airport is about 30 minutes away, and MidAmerica St. Louis Airport near Belleville has some airline service ; Interstate 255 passes nearby and connects to I-64 and I-55/I-70 ; Metro buses connect to MetroLink light rail in East St. Louis

## IL|McHenry
intro: McHenry is a city of about 27,000 people on the Fox River in McHenry County, about 46 miles northwest of Chicago, in an area of natural lakes, rolling hills and bogs left by glaciers.
family: Six Flags Great America in Gurnee, about 40 minutes away, is a large amusement park open April to November ; Fiesta Days, an eleven-day festival each July, has music, a parade, a carnival and car shows ; The McHenry Outdoor Theater is a drive-in open from March to December
outdoors: Moraine Hills State Park covers about 2,200 acres around Lake Defiance with miles of hiking trails ; Volo Bog State Natural Area has a boardwalk into a quaking bog that is a National Natural Landmark ; Lake Geneva, Wisconsin, about 30 minutes away, is a resort town with boating and water sports
food: Downtown McHenry has three districts, Green Street, Riverside Drive and Main Street, with local shops and restaurants ; The McHenry Riverwalk follows Boone Creek and the Fox River and includes a concert venue ; Chicago, about an hour and 20 minutes away, is known for jazz, blues and big-city nightlife
sports: Chicago has teams in every major pro league, including two Major League Baseball teams ; Milwaukee, about an hour and a half away, has the Brewers and the Bucks ; The Chicago Marathon is one of the world's major marathons
cost: Housing costs are below the U.S. average, and groceries and utilities are slightly below average.
climate: Winters are cold and snowy, with January highs around 30 and about 35 inches of snow. Summers are warm, with July highs in the mid 80s.
vets: A VA clinic is in McHenry ; The Captain James A. Lovell Federal Health Care Center, the main VA hospital for the area, is in North Chicago ; Naval Station Great Lakes, home of the Navy's only boot camp, is about 40 minutes away
around: O'Hare International Airport is about an hour and 20 minutes away, and Milwaukee Mitchell is the other major airport ; Chicago is about an hour and 20 minutes away and Milwaukee about an hour and a half ; Metra trains run on weekdays from McHenry to downtown Chicago, and Illinois Routes 31 and 120 are the main roads

## KS|Wichita
intro: Wichita is the largest city in Kansas, with about 398,000 people, on the Arkansas River. It is known for aircraft manufacturing, with several major aviation companies operating in the city.
family: The Sedgwick County Zoo covers about 247 acres with more than 3,000 animals ; Old Cowtown Museum shows the city's early history ; The Keeper of the Plains, a 44-foot steel sculpture, stands where the Arkansas and Little Arkansas rivers meet
outdoors: Cheney State Park, about an hour away on Cheney Reservoir, has boat ramps, swimming beaches, campgrounds and a marina ; El Dorado State Park, about an hour away in the Flint Hills, is the largest state park in Kansas ; Tallgrass Prairie National Preserve, about an hour and 35 minutes away, has more than 40 miles of hiking trails
food: Old Town, part of downtown, is a 50-acre district of restaurants, bars, nightclubs, shops and a movie theater ; Intrust Bank Arena and Century II host concerts and shows
sports: Wichita has Double-A minor league baseball in the Texas League ; The Wichita Thunder play ECHL hockey at Intrust Bank Arena ; Wichita State University is in the city
cost: Housing costs are far below the U.S. average, and groceries are a little below average.
climate: Hot, humid summers with July highs in the low 90s, and cold, dry winters with January highs in the mid 40s. Spring and summer bring frequent thunderstorms, and snow averages about 13 inches a year.
vets: The VA medical center for the region is on East Kellogg Drive in Wichita ; McConnell Air Force Base, home of the 22nd Air Refueling Wing, is on the southeast side of the city
around: Wichita's Eisenhower National Airport is the largest airport in Kansas ; Oklahoma City is about two hours and 50 minutes away and Kansas City about three and a half hours ; Interstate 35 and U.S. 54 meet in Wichita, and the city has a public bus system
