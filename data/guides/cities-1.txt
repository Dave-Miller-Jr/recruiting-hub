## AR|Arkadelphia
intro: Arkadelphia is a college town of about 10,400 people and the county seat of Clark County. It sits at the foothills of the Ouachita Mountains on Interstate 30 between Little Rock and Texarkana.
family: Henderson State University and Ouachita Baptist University are both in the city. ; The Arkadelphia Arts Center hosts exhibits, productions and educational workshops. ; The Reynolds Science Center Planetarium is on the Henderson State campus.
outdoors: DeGray Lake, on the Caddo River, has 208 miles of shoreline and 13,800 acres of water. ; DeGray Lake Resort State Park offers fishing, swimming, boating, hiking, horseback riding and an 18-hole golf course. ; Hot Springs National Park, under an hour away, has hiking trails and camping areas.
sports: Henderson State's Reddies compete in NCAA Division II in the Great American Conference. ; Henderson State and Ouachita Baptist share the longest rivalry in Division II football, the Battle of the Ravine, which began in 1895.
cost: BestPlaces index figures put housing in Arkadelphia far below the US average, with groceries, utilities and other everyday costs somewhat below it.
climate: Summers are hot and humid and winters are generally mild to cool. Typical daily highs are about 55°F in January and about 94°F in July.
vets: The VA has a clinic in Hot Springs, under an hour away. ; The main VA medical center for central Arkansas is in Little Rock, about an hour and 15 minutes away.
around: Interstate 30 runs through town. ; Hot Springs is under an hour away by car and Little Rock about an hour and 15 minutes. ; Texarkana is about an hour and a half away.
