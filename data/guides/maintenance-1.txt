## KS|Parsons
intro: Parsons is a small city of about 9,600 people in southeast Kansas, the largest in Labette County, where U.S. Routes 59 and 400 meet. It is home to Labette Community College.
family: Forest Park, the city's largest park, hosts the Katy Days festival each Memorial Day weekend and a summer concert series ; Marvel Park hosts the Fourth of July fireworks ; The city has a municipal swimming pool and an arts center in the former Carnegie library
outdoors: Big Hill Lake, a Corps of Engineers reservoir about half an hour away, has fishing, boating, camping and a 17-mile horse trail ; Lake Parsons, about half an hour away, has fishing, camping, boating and a beach on about 1,000 acres of public land ; Fall River State Park, about an hour and twenty minutes away, has hiking trails, camping and boating
food: Local dining runs from barbecue to classic diners ; Pittsburg, about 50 minutes away, holds an art walk on Broadway several times a year and the Little Balkans Days festival every Labor Day weekend
sports: Labette Community College Cardinals teams compete in the Kansas Jayhawk Community College Conference ; Marvel Park's 2,000-seat stadium hosts Parsons High School Vikings football and college baseball
climate: Four seasons. Average highs are about 90 in July and the low 40s in January, with about 43 inches of precipitation a year. May and June are the wettest months.
around: Joplin Regional Airport in Missouri, about an hour and 10 minutes away, has commercial flights ; Pittsburg is about 50 minutes away and Joplin about an hour and 15 minutes ; Tulsa is about two hours and 20 minutes away and Wichita about two and a half hours

## AK|Soldotna
intro: Soldotna is a small city of about 4,300 people on the Kenai River in the middle of Alaska's Kenai Peninsula. It is the borough seat and the service and shopping hub for the central peninsula.
family: Soldotna Creek Park has river access, a community playground, picnic pavilions and an amphitheater ; The visitor center at the Kenai River bridge has wildlife displays, with the Homestead Museum nearby ; The Kenai Watershed Forum runs a summer camp for kids ages 6 to 12 from Soldotna Creek Park
outdoors: The Kenai River holds all five species of Pacific salmon, and the city has several public river-access parks ; The 1.9-million-acre Kenai National Wildlife Refuge starts at the edge of town, with a visitor center on Ski Hill Road ; Tsalteshi Trails has more than 25 kilometers of trails for cross-country skiing in winter and hiking and mountain biking in summer
sports: The Kenai River Brown Bears, a junior hockey team in the North American Hockey League, play at the Soldotna Regional Sports Complex
cost: Overall cost of living is close to the U.S. average and below the Alaska average. Alaska has no state income tax, and residents who have lived in the state for a full calendar year receive the annual Permanent Fund dividend.
climate: Summers are cool, with July highs in the mid 60s. Winters are long and snowy but not especially cold for the latitude, with January highs around 20.
vets: A VA outpatient clinic is in Kenai, about 20 minutes away ; A Vet Center satellite office is in Soldotna
around: Kenai Municipal Airport, about 20 minutes away, has scheduled commercial flights ; Homer is about an hour and 50 minutes south on the Sterling Highway ; Anchorage is about three hours and 20 minutes by road

## AR|Texarkana
intro: Texarkana is a twin city on the Arkansas and Texas state line, with about 29,000 people on the Arkansas side. The federal courthouse and post office downtown sits directly on the state line.
family: The courthouse and post office that straddles the state line is the city's best-known landmark ; Millwood State Park has camping, hiking and bike trails and a marina with boat rentals ; The area has three colleges, including Texas A&M University-Texarkana
outdoors: Wright Patman Lake, a Corps of Engineers reservoir southwest of town, has nine Corps parks for boating and fishing ; Atlanta State Park sits on the south shore of Wright Patman Lake ; Millwood Lake and its state park offer fishing, camping and trails
food: Shreveport, about 72 miles south, has held the Mudbug Madness crawfish festival since 1984 ; Dallas, about 180 miles southwest, is the nearest very large city for dining and nightlife
sports: Texas A&M University-Texarkana is the local four-year university ; War Memorial Stadium in Little Rock, about 143 miles northeast, hosts an Arkansas Razorbacks football game each year ; Little Rock also has Double-A baseball, the Arkansas Travelers
cost: People whose permanent home is inside the Texarkana, Arkansas city limits are exempt from Arkansas individual income tax.
climate: Summers are hot and humid, with highs in the low 90s in July and August. Winters are mild, with January highs in the mid 50s, and snow is rare.
vets: A VA outpatient clinic is in Texarkana, Arkansas ; Overton Brooks VA Medical Center is in Shreveport, about 72 miles away ; Red River Army Depot is about 18 miles west of town
around: Texarkana Regional Airport has regional jet service to Dallas/Fort Worth ; Interstate 30 and Interstate 49 meet here, along with U.S. 59, 67, 71 and 82 ; Amtrak's daily Texas Eagle stops at Union Station on the state line, and the city has a local bus system

## AZ|Bullhead City
intro: Bullhead City is a desert city of about 41,000 people on the Colorado River at the south end of Lake Mohave. Laughlin, Nevada is directly across the river.
family: Rotary Park, a 300-acre riverfront park, has beaches, a skate park, a dog park and a lighted sports complex ; Ken Fovargue Park has a public pool with a water slide, a playground, ball fields and a bike trail ; The Colorado River Museum, open September through May, covers regional history
outdoors: Lake Mohave is a 67-mile stretch of the Colorado River for swimming, boating, kayaking and fishing ; Katherine Landing, the largest marina on Lake Mohave, has launch ramps and campgrounds inside Lake Mead National Recreation Area ; Oatman, an old gold-mining town on Route 66 in the Black Mountains, is known for its wooden sidewalks and wild burros
food: Laughlin's casino resorts are just across the river ; Las Vegas, the closest large city, is 97 miles away
sports: Rotary Park hosts a large youth soccer tournament every February ; The city hosts senior softball tournaments each spring and fall
cost: Overall cost of living is about the U.S. average and below the Arizona average, and housing costs are below the U.S. average.
climate: Summers are very hot, with average highs of about 107 to 111 from June through August and heat waves above 120. Winters are mild and sunny, with highs in the mid 60s to about 70, and rain is light.
vets: VA outpatient clinics are in Kingman and Lake Havasu City ; The VA Southern Nevada medical center is in the Las Vegas area
around: Harry Reid International Airport in Las Vegas is the nearest major airport, with shuttle companies serving Bullhead City ; Kingman is 34 miles east ; State Routes 95 and 68 connect to Interstate 40, and the city has a local bus system

## AZ|Kingman
intro: Kingman is a city of about 33,000 people in northwestern Arizona and the seat of Mohave County. It calls itself the Heart of Historic Route 66 and sits where Interstate 40 and U.S. 93 meet.
family: The Arizona Route 66 Museum is in the historic Powerhouse building ; The downtown train station, a Mission Revival building, houses a model-railroad museum ; Hualapai Mountain Park has cabins, camping and picnic areas in the pines
outdoors: Hualapai Mountain Park has more than 10 miles of hiking trails ; Hualapai Peak, at 8,417 feet, is the highest point in Mohave County ; The 40,000-acre Wabayuma Peak Wilderness lies in the southern Hualapai Mountains
food: Laughlin, a Colorado River resort town, is about 34 miles away ; Las Vegas, about 107 miles northwest on U.S. 93, has big-city dining, shows and nightlife
sports: Las Vegas has the Raiders, the Golden Knights, the Aces and Triple-A baseball ; Las Vegas also hosts NASCAR races and a Formula One grand prix each year
cost: Overall cost of living is about the U.S. average and below the Arizona average, and housing costs are well below the U.S. average.
climate: At about 3,500 feet, Kingman is milder than Phoenix or the river cities. July highs are near 99 with cooler nights, and winters bring highs in the upper 50s, freezing nights and occasional snow.
vets: A VA clinic is in Kingman ; The Northern Arizona VA medical center is in Prescott
around: Harry Reid International Airport in Las Vegas, about 104 miles northwest, is the nearest major airport ; Interstate 40 runs east to Flagstaff, about 147 miles ; Amtrak's Southwest Chief stops daily downtown, and the city runs a local bus system

## AZ|Mesa
intro: Mesa is a city of about 504,000 people in the East Valley of the Phoenix metropolitan area, in the Sonoran Desert.
family: Red Mountain Park covers more than 1,100 acres with a lake, playgrounds and sports courts ; Museums include the Arizona Museum of Natural History, the Mesa Historical Museum and the i.d.e.a. Museum for kids ; The Mesa Arts Center downtown is one of the city's main cultural attractions
outdoors: Lost Dutchman State Park, on the west side of Superstition Mountain, has hiking and mountain bike trails ; Saguaro Lake, a Salt River reservoir off State Route 87, is popular for bass fishing and boating ; Canyon Lake, up the Apache Trail, has boating, camping and hiking beside the Superstition Wilderness
food: The Mesa Arts Center and Mesa Amphitheater host concerts and shows ; Mesa is part of the Phoenix metro area, with big-city dining and nightlife close by
sports: The Chicago Cubs hold spring training at Sloan Park ; The Athletics hold spring training at Hohokam Stadium ; Arizona State University has its Polytechnic campus in southeast Mesa
cost: Overall cost of living is slightly above the U.S. average, with utilities running below average.
climate: A hot desert climate. Summers are very hot, with July highs near 106, and winters are mild, with January highs in the upper 60s. Rain is about 11 inches a year.
vets: VA clinics are in Mesa and neighboring Gilbert ; The Phoenix VA medical center is on Indian School Road in Phoenix ; Luke Air Force Base is west of Phoenix
around: Mesa Gateway Airport is in southeast Mesa, with fewer flights than Phoenix Sky Harbor ; Phoenix Sky Harbor, an American Airlines hub, is just east of downtown Phoenix ; Valley Metro light rail runs along Main Street through west and central Mesa

## CA|Imperial
intro: Imperial is a small city of about 20,000 people in California's Imperial Valley farming region, four miles north of El Centro.
family: The California Mid-Winter Fair runs for 10 days in Imperial each year around the end of February ; Imperial Market Days is a free downtown event series that starts each October ; El Centro's city parks include Bucklin Park, Countryside Park and Legacy Park
outdoors: The Imperial Sand Dunes Recreation Area, also called Glamis, is open to off-road vehicles and camping ; The Salton Sea is a major stop for migrating birds on the Pacific Flyway
food: The valley's farms grow winter vegetables such as lettuce and carrots ; The Mid-Winter Fairgrounds in Imperial hosts festivals, concerts and car races through the year
sports: Naval Air Facility El Centro is the winter training home of the Blue Angels, who traditionally open their season with an air show there
climate: A hot desert climate with long, extremely hot summers and mild winters. Highs are about 104 in July and August and in the upper 60s in December and January, with about 3 inches of rain a year.
vets: Naval Air Facility El Centro is a few miles away ; A VA clinic serves the Imperial Valley in El Centro, and the VA medical center is in San Diego
around: Imperial County Airport is in Imperial, and the nearest large airport is San Diego ; Interstate 8 runs west to San Diego, about 113 miles, and east to Yuma, Arizona ; Imperial Valley Transit runs local buses

## CA|San Bernardino
intro: San Bernardino is a city of about 222,000 people and the county seat of San Bernardino County, a regional hub of Southern California's Inland Empire at the foot of the San Bernardino Mountains.
family: Cal State San Bernardino has an accredited art museum and an observatory with public viewing nights ; Lake Arrowhead Village holds events all year, including free summer concerts ; The Western Regional Little League Championships are played in San Bernardino each August
outdoors: San Bernardino National Forest, more than 800,000 acres, is headquartered in the city ; Big Bear Lake in the mountains has fishing, hiking and skiing at Snow Summit and Bear Mountain ; The Rim of the World Highway climbs from the city past Lake Arrowhead to Big Bear Lake
food: Downtown hosts the Route 66 Rendezvous each September ; Big Bear Lake has held an Oktoberfest every year since 1970
sports: The Inland Empire 66ers play minor league baseball at San Manuel Stadium ; Cal State San Bernardino Coyotes teams compete in NCAA Division II
cost: Overall cost of living is above the U.S. average, mostly because of housing, but typical home costs are well below the California average.
climate: Summers are hot and dry, with July and August highs in the mid to upper 90s. Winters are mild, with highs in the upper 60s and chilly mornings, and about 16 inches of rain a year.
vets: The VA medical center for the region is in neighboring Loma Linda ; VA clinics are also in Redlands, Rancho Cucamonga, Victorville, Corona, Hemet and Palm Desert ; March Air Reserve Base is near Riverside
around: Ontario International Airport is about 18 miles west of downtown, and San Bernardino International Airport has limited airline service ; Metrolink trains run daily to Los Angeles Union Station ; Interstates 10 and 215 and State Route 210 meet here

## CO|Englewood
intro: Englewood is a city of about 34,000 people immediately south of Denver in the South Platte River valley, part of the Denver metropolitan area.
family: The Denver Museum of Nature and Science has a planetarium and a play area for young children ; The Denver Zoo covers 80 acres in Denver's City Park ; Englewood's Civic Center holds the public library and the Museum of Outdoor Arts
outdoors: The South Platte River trail runs through the city ; Cherry Creek State Park has an 880-acre reservoir, a swim beach and about 35 miles of trails ; Chatfield State Park centers on a 1,400-acre reservoir and is known for birdwatching
food: Downtown Denver and its historic LoDo district are a light rail ride away ; Red Rocks Amphitheatre has hosted concerts since 1941
sports: Denver has the Broncos, Nuggets, Avalanche, Rockies and Rapids ; The Broncos play at Empower Field at Mile High, and the Nuggets and Avalanche play at Ball Arena
cost: Colorado has a flat state income tax.
climate: Four seasons in a dry climate. Highs are in the upper 80s in July and the low 40s in January.
vets: Buckley Space Force Base is in nearby Aurora
around: Denver International Airport is a United Airlines hub ; RTD light rail runs from Englewood station to downtown Denver ; Interstate 25 is a few miles east
