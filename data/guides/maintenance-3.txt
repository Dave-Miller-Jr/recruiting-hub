## KY|Jamestown
intro: Jamestown is a small town of about 1,900 people and the seat of Russell County in south-central Kentucky, right beside Lake Cumberland.
family: Lake Cumberland State Resort Park, about four miles south, has lodging, camping, a marina and disc golf ; The town has a branch of the county public library ; Lexington, about an hour and 50 minutes away, holds a holiday lights display at the Kentucky Horse Park each winter
outdoors: Lake Cumberland has about 1,255 miles of shoreline and is popular for houseboating ; Several Kentucky record fish, including brown trout and striped bass, have come from Lake Cumberland ; The state resort park marina offers boating and fishing access
food: Somerset, about 40 minutes away, hosts the annual Master Musicians Festival ; Nashville, known as Music City, is about two hours and 45 minutes away
sports: Bowling Green, about an hour and 40 minutes away, has Hot Rods minor league baseball and Western Kentucky University Hilltoppers teams ; The Kentucky Derby runs each May in Louisville, about two hours and 20 minutes away
cost: Housing costs are far below the U.S. average, and groceries and utilities are also below average.
climate: Hot, humid summers with July highs in the mid 80s, and mild to cool winters with January highs in the mid 40s. Snow is light, about 5 inches a year.
vets: A VA clinic is in Somerset, about 40 minutes away ; The VA medical center is in Lexington, about an hour and 50 minutes away
around: Blue Grass Airport in Lexington is about an hour and 50 minutes away, and Louisville's airport is about two hours and 20 minutes ; U.S. Route 127 and the Cumberland Parkway run through neighboring Russell Springs, about 7 minutes away

## TN|Nashville

sports: Nashville has three major pro teams: the Titans, the Predators and Nashville SC ; The Titans play at Nissan Stadium

around: Nashville's international airport (BNA) is about 12 minutes from downtown, with a city bus link ; Louisville and Knoxville are each about three hours and 15 minutes away, and Memphis about four hours ; Interstate 40 runs through the city

## PA|West Mifflin

outdoors: The Great Allegheny Passage, a 150-mile rail trail for hiking and cycling from Pittsburgh to Cumberland, Maryland, passes near Kennywood ; Ohiopyle State Park on the Youghiogheny River, along the same trail, is a popular whitewater destination ; Fallingwater, the Frank Lloyd Wright house, is in the same area

sports: The Steelers, Penguins and Pirates all play in Pittsburgh, about 20 minutes away ; Pittsburgh colleges include the University of Pittsburgh, Duquesne and Carnegie Mellon
cost: Housing costs in West Mifflin are far below the U.S. average, and groceries and utilities are about average. Pennsylvania has a flat state income tax, and most municipalities also levy a local earned income tax.
climate: Winters are cold and snowy, with January highs in the mid 30s. Summers are warm, with July highs in the low 80s, and spring and fall are mild.

## NM|Farmington
intro: Farmington is a city of about 46,600 people in San Juan County, where the San Juan, Animas and La Plata rivers meet. It is the commercial hub for northwestern New Mexico and the Four Corners region.
family: The annual Riverfest brings music, food, a 10K and 5K run and walk, riverside trail walks and river raft rides. ; Aztec Ruins National Monument, about 25 minutes away, preserves Ancestral Puebloan structures from the 12th and 13th centuries. ; The Farmington Public Library holds about 200,000 items.
outdoors: Fishing is popular on the San Juan and Animas rivers, Navajo Lake and Lake Farmington. ; Navajo Lake State Park has a 15,600-acre reservoir with smallmouth bass, trout and kokanee salmon. ; Piñon Hills is a city-owned municipal golf course.
food: Durango's Main Avenue historic district, about 1 hour 10 minutes away, has galleries, boutiques, restaurants and bars. ; Albuquerque, about 3.5 hours away, is the nearest big-city restaurant scene, with New Mexican and global cuisine.
sports: Farmington has hosted the Connie Mack World Series baseball tournament every August at Ricketts Park since 1965. ; The Iron Horse bicycle road race, founded in 1972, is held each year in Durango, about 1 hour 10 minutes away. ; The Shiprock Marathon and Relay has been run in Shiprock since 1984.
cost: BestPlaces index figures put Farmington's housing far below the US average, with groceries and utilities also below average.
climate: Farmington has a semi-arid climate with cold winters and hot summers. Typical daily highs are near 41°F in January and near 90°F in July, and the area gets only about 8 inches of precipitation a year.
vets: The VA lists a clinic in Farmington. ; There is also a VA outpatient clinic in Durango, Colorado, about 1 hour 10 minutes away.
around: Durango-La Plata County Airport in Colorado has year-round regional airline service. ; Albuquerque International Sunport, New Mexico's primary airport, is about 3.5 hours away. ; U.S. Highway 64 runs east-west through town, and Red Apple Transit provides local bus service.

## WY|Evanston
intro: Evanston is a city of about 12,000 people in the southwest corner of Wyoming, near the Utah border. It is the county seat of Uinta County and began in 1868 as a railroad town.
family: The city Recreation Center has a swimming pool and youth classes and sports. ; The Uinta County Museum is on Depot Square downtown, in a building first built as a Carnegie library in 1906. ; City parks have basketball and tennis courts, soccer fields and sand volleyball.

sports: The annual Cowboy Days brings a professional rodeo to the Uinta County Fairgrounds. ; Park City, Utah, about an hour and 10 minutes away, has two major ski resorts.
cost: BestPlaces index figures put housing in Evanston well below the US average, with groceries, utilities and other everyday costs also below it.
climate: Winters are long, cold and dry, and summers are short and warm. Typical daily highs are about 33°F in January and 81°F in July, with about 47 inches of snow a year.
vets: The nearest VA medical center listed is in Salt Lake City, about an hour and a half away. ; The Salt Lake City VA system also lists a clinic in South Ogden, Utah.
around: Interstate 80 runs past town. ; Salt Lake City and its international airport are about an hour and a half away by car. ; Park City, Utah, is about an hour and 10 minutes away.
