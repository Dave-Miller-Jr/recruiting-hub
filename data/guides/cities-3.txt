## CA|Ridgecrest
intro: Ridgecrest is a city of about 28,000 people in northeastern Kern County, in the Indian Wells Valley along U.S. Route 395. It sits next to Naval Air Weapons Station China Lake, the area's main employer.
family: The Maturango Museum covers the natural and cultural history of the northern Mojave Desert and leads tours to Little Petroglyph Canyon. ; The Ridgecrest Petroglyph Festival celebrates the area's rock art and native culture each year. ; Cerro Coso Community College has its main campus near Ridgecrest.

climate: Summers are hot and extremely dry with cool nights, and the area gets under 5 inches of rain a year. Typical daily highs are about 60°F in December and about 105°F in July.

around: The Lancaster and Palmdale area is about 82 miles away, roughly an hour and a half to two hours by road. ; Bakersfield is about 110 miles away, a bit over two hours. ; Lone Pine, near the Alabama Hills in the eastern Sierra, is roughly an hour and a half away.
